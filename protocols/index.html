    Protocols#

    Overview#

    Protocols are standardized rules and procedures that enable communication between devices over networks or serial connections. This page serves as the parent index for all protocol-related topics.

    Protocol Categories#

    Authentication#

    Authentication protocols manage identity verification, access control, and centralized login for users and devices. They are widely used in enterprise networks, industrial systems, and control centers.

    Common authentication protocols:

    • LDAP
    • RADIUS

    See: Protocols/Authentication/

    Data#

    Data protocols define the rules for exchanging operational or measurement data between devices. They are critical for industrial control, SCADA, and substation automation systems.

    Common data protocols:

    • DNP3
    • Modbus
    • IEC 61850

    See: Protocols/Data/

    Network#

    Network protocols govern device discovery, addressing, routing, and topology management. They ensure reliable and deterministic communication across local and wide-area networks.

    Common network protocols:

    • ARP
    • DHCP
    • RSTP

    See: Protocols/Network/

    Security#

    Security protocols provide confidentiality, integrity, and authentication for communications. They are essential for protecting networks, devices, and sensitive data.

    Common security protocols:

    • IPSec
    • SSH
    • TLS / SSL

    See: Protocols/Security/

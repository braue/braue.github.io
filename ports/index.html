    Ports#

    Overview#

    Ports are physical interfaces used for communication between intelligent devices. This page serves as the parent index for all port-related topics.

    Port Categories#

    Copper#

    Copper ports use electrical signaling over twisted-pair or coaxial conductors. They are common for short- to medium-distance communication and are widely used in networking and industrial equipment.

    Common copper port types:

    • RJ45
    • M12
    • BNC coax

    See: Ports/Copper/

    Fiber#

    Fiber ports use optical signaling through glass or plastic strands. They are used for high-speed communication and long-distance links, offering strong immunity to EMI and electrical noise.

    Common fiber port types:

    • LC
    • SC
    • ST
    • SFP / SFP+
    • QSFP / QSFP+

    See: Ports/Fiber/

    Serial#

    Serial ports use electrical signaling with defined voltage levels (RS-232, RS-485, RS-422) and support point-to-point or multi-drop communication. They remain common in industrial control, SCADA, and embedded systems.

    Common serial port types:

    • DB9
    • DB25

    Common serial wiring/signaling standards:

    • RS-485
    • RS-422
    • RS-232

    See: Ports/Serial/
